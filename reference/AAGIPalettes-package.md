# AAGIPalettes: AAGI Colours and Colour Palettes

Colour palettes based on the official Analytics for the Australian
Grains Industry ('AAGI') comms guide and others designed to work
harmoniously with the official 'AAGI' colours while being colour vision
deficient (CVD) friendly.
